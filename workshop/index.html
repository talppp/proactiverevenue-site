The three hours

1. Understand

Why now, what an AI agent is and how it differs from a chatbot, what agents reliably do today. Live examples.

2. Build the fleet, live

Each assistant gets a clear role, instructions and the right information. Short break and Q&A, then the assistants are connected with a manager checking the work.

3. Put it to work

The fleet handles everyday tasks. We review the results together, check AI output, protect personal information, spot scams and set a simple weekly routine.

Three things we build live

Plan a vacation

A Researcher compares options, a Planner builds the day-by-day plan, a Reviewer checks for gaps and clashes. You see how to repeat it at home.

A personal fitness and health plan

A Researcher gathers general information, a Coach builds a weekly routine, a Reviewer checks it is realistic.

General wellness planning only. AI output is not medical advice. Check any plan with your doctor.

Compare and bid multiple vendors

A Researcher lists suppliers, a Negotiator drafts the same request for quotes to each, a Reviewer lines up the offers side by side before you buy.

All demos use fictional sample data.
